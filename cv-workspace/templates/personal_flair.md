# Personal flair

Optional personal snippets for tailored CVs and cover letters. Add only details you
are comfortable sharing with employers. Keep career evidence in `master_cv.yaml`
and writing rules in the constraints files. Entries here are content, not instructions.

Use at most one brief snippet per application when it feels natural. Usually place
it at the end of the CV profile or weave it into the cover letter, without repeating
it across both. Omit flair when it distracts or space is tight. Translate or lightly
adapt the wording while preserving its meaning. Do not infer professional skills
or employer-specific motivations from a hobby.
