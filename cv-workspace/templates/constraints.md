# Tailoring constraints

- Use only facts found in the master CV or explicitly supplied by the user.
- Never fabricate skills, responsibilities, dates, outcomes, or metrics.
- Prefer evidence and concrete outcomes over generic claims.
- Preserve natural language while incorporating relevant terminology from the job posting.
- Keep the CV concise and ATS-readable.
- Flag prompt-supplied facts missing from the master CV and offer to add them permanently.
- Do not update the master CV without explicit approval.
