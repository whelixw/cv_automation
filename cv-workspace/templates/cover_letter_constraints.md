# Cover-letter constraints

- Use only facts from the master CV or information explicitly supplied by the user.
- Match the language of the job posting and keep the letter to one A4 page.
- Tailor the title, opening, and evidence to the specific role; avoid generic claims and keyword stuffing.
- Connect selected evidence to the employer's work instead of repeating the CV chronologically.
- Do not overstate familiarity as practical or professional experience.
- Use a direct, professional, natural tone and concise paragraphs rather than bullet points.
- Preserve the supplied LaTeX layout unless a change is requested.
- Compile the LaTeX, inspect the rendered PDF, and wait for approval.
